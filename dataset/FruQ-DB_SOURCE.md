# FruQ-DB source and class mapping

Source: Abayomi-Alli Olusola and Robertas Damaševičius, *Fruit Quality Datasets (FruQ-DB)*, Zenodo record 7224690, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7224690

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). Cite the dataset authors and retain this attribution when redistributing the images or derivatives.

Original archive checksum: MD5 `1a942c2d49dc302bacef155561e1f9a8`.

Original archive classes are mapped directly as `Fresh` → `segar`, `Mild` → `kurang_segar`, and `Rotten` → `busuk`. Images are mixed fruit types and are stored in the three condition folders directly under `dataset/`; the archive's single-condition folders do not identify fruit type per image. The archive describes 2,101 Rotten samples. The ZIP's Rotten folder has 2,101 PNG images and one non-image shortcut; the shortcut is excluded.
